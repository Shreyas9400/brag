Two college mates. Two promising careers — one in a senior management position with the Tata Group, the other in banking.

But in the 1980s, they shared a bigger dream: to build something of their own.

What began with a small family restaurant and a leap of faith has today grown into a ₹450 crore hospitality enterprise, spanning multiple brands and businesses.

This is more than a story of growth. It is the story of two friends who started with one restaurant and built a legacy.
